HelpMemory

A calm caregiving companion for early forgetfulness and mild cognitive decline

About HelpMemory

HelpMemory is an elder-care app for families supporting parents with early forgetfulness or mild cognitive decline. It focuses on everyday routines, dementia-safe support, and emotional connection for households living with memory loss.

HelpMemory does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. It is a memory-support tool that complements professional dementia care.

Package: com.helpmemory.helpmemory

Medical disclaimer: Family setup is required. The app does not replace emergency services or professional care for people living with dementia.

Key features

1. Gentle daily reminders

Set simple routines for older parents: drink water, take a walk, take medicine. A distinctive Capsule 3D interface with oversized typography helps people with mild dementia read clearly and stay more independent — while reducing caregiver stress.

2. SOS GPS & wandering support

Define a home safety area. If a parent with dementia feels lost or disoriented, one tap on SOS can share a real-time GPS location with family and open Google Maps for guidance home.

Important — how rescue works: To protect privacy and phone safety, HelpMemory does not run in the background or track location automatically. Rescue works only when:
  • HelpMemory is open on the user’s phone
  • The user moves more than 500 meters from the registered home location
When both conditions are met, an emergency screen appears. One tap shares the exact location with family. If the app is closed, rescue features do not work.
3. Memory stimulation

Load nostalgic music, family stories, or YouTube videos to ease anxiety. Gentle cognitive cues help older adults in early Alzheimer stages recall familiar moments and feel calmer.

4. Family memory album

Store family photos and gatherings. Familiar images help parents with memory loss feel connected and loved — even when caregivers cannot be present.

5. Quick notes for forgetfulness

A simple note tool for daily tasks. Caregivers or older adults can save important details to reduce worry from forgetfulness and support mild cognitive decline.

For caregivers

HelpMemory is built around one promise: “Even when life is busy, I am still here for you.”

It helps families stay connected through routines, shared memories, and a clear SOS path — without pretending to be a medical or emergency-response system.
