HelpMemory is a family caregiving app for older adults with early forgetfulness or mild cognitive decline. We respect your privacy and design location features carefully so they support safety without hidden tracking.

1. What HelpMemory is (and is not)

HelpMemory helps families with daily reminders, memory cues, family albums, quick notes, and an on-screen SOS location share when the app is open. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and it does not replace emergency services or professional dementia care.

2. Data stored on your device

Reminders, notes, family album content you add, and home-location settings are intended to stay on the device for caregiving use. We do not operate a cloud account system that requires you to create a personal profile to use core features.

3. Location & SOS (important)

HelpMemory may request location permission so SOS and home-area safety features can work. To protect privacy and phone safety:

  • HelpMemory does not run continuous background location tracking.
  • Rescue / SOS sharing is designed to work while the app is open.
  • When the user is farther than about 500 meters from the registered home location and the app is open, an emergency screen may appear.
  • With one tap, the user’s current location can be shared with family contacts you configure and Google Maps can open for guidance home.
  • If the app is closed, rescue features do not operate.

We do not sell location data. Location is used for the safety features you choose to use.

4. Photos, media & notes

Family album photos and notes you add are for in-app caregiving support. The app may also open or play nostalgic music, family stories, or YouTube videos you choose. YouTube content remains owned by its uploaders under YouTube’s terms; HelpMemory does not claim ownership of that content.

5. No required login for core use

We do not require name, email, or phone signup solely to use the app’s core memory-support tools. Contact details you enter for family SOS sharing are used for those safety features you set up.

6. Advertising

The app may show ads to remain free. We do not sell your personal notes, photos, or location history as a data product. Ads, if shown, may be provided by Google AdMob and are not based on a personal caregiving profile we create about you.

7. Permissions

The app only requests permissions needed for features you use — such as location for SOS/home-area support, storage/media access for albums or offline content, and related system capabilities. We do not access unrelated personal files on your device.

8. Children

HelpMemory is intended for caregivers and older adults. It is not directed at children under 13.

9. Contact / requests

If you have privacy questions, or believe specific content should be reviewed, contact us by email or through the support channel on the Google Play or App Store listing where you installed HelpMemory. We will review promptly.

In short: HelpMemory is built to support families living with early memory loss. Your caregiving notes and album content are meant to stay on your device. Location is for on-app SOS and home-area safety — not hidden background tracking. We do not sell your personal caregiving data.
